The Box Model

Understanding the box model means learning how to control "physical volume" in the digital world, ensuring your design is not only beautiful but also logically rigorous.

grid_view THE GROWTH METAPHOR

The Box Model defines the "living space" of these objects. By controlling Padding,Border,and Margin,you determine whether an element is a compact core or a lord with a vast territory.

potted_plant

Padding

The inner space of an element. It is the distance between the element's content (like text or an image) and its border.

shield

Border

The actual frame surrounding your picture. It sits exactly between the Padding and the Margin.

space_dashboard

Margin

The outer space around the element. It is the distance between your picture frame and other objects nearby
